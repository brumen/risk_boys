SYNOPSIS

Recurring characters:

Shapley Morton (SM): is a large Wall Street brokerage
company. It has a standard pyramid shape management
structure. All characters, except for Roger, work for this
company.

Sonny: Sonny is a good looking, well kept guy, who shaves
daily and wears a white shirt and a tie at all the time. As
a result of the lavish money in early 2000s, he never
developed a deep understanding of the commodity space and
the factors that drive it. He is using profanity liberally.
He works for SM.

Roger: Roger is from Hemispheric Energy, a gas and oil
exploration company. He is a typical non-Wall Street
manager, overly positive and exaggerating. His dress style
is somewhat more casual as Sonny's. He has an overcomb, and
is slightly overweight. Very shallow and vain.

Brett: Brett is Sonny's boss. He is a polished New England
executive with a great haircut, white shirt and a tie.
Brett's corner office is on a high floor of SM building and
has great view of NY. On his desk are a photo of his wife
and 2 children, a model schooner. Behind the desk on the
wall is the .  

Dr. Tang: Dr. Tang is a strat in the organization, I.e. he
works on the mathematical models. He is always referred to
as Dr. Tang, as if he doesn't have a first name. His team
includes people with advanced PhD math, science, and
computer-science degrees. He speaks carefully and precisely.
He has at least six really large
screens in front of him, full of spreadsheets, coding
editors etc., and is running all sorts of analysis, coding.

Igor: Igor works in the risk management department. He
wears somewhat large, clumsy glasses. He tries to take care
of his appearance, but can't quite pull it all together - a
large stain on his shirt or something always gives it away.

Amisha: An associate of Sonny who works beneath him. She is
very smart, but pursued a career in finance because her
family regarded it highly. She slowly grows disillusioned
with not being promoted, even though she has not clearly
defined what advancement would look like to her.

Joshua: Another associate of Sonny. Like Amisha, he went
into finance for lack of motivation elsewhere and does not
really like it, though it keeps him in good standing with
his family. He tries to be helpful, but often makes mistakes.
He is humble rather than arrogant, unlike his boss Sonny.
Secretly, he wants to pursue more creative endeavors.

SONNY'S DESK

terminals in front of him. A dispatcher phone console. Other
traders/sales -people around him talking on the phones,
browsing Bloomberg, doing spreadsheet analysis.

Amisha and Joshua are sitting at their respective desks.
Joshua is trying to work on some spreadsheets, but Amisha is
just browsing her cell phone with only e-mail open on her
two screens. The camera switches between Roger, who is in an
office at Hemispheric, and Sonny, who is at his trading
desk. Sometimes the camera stays on Roger or Sonny, and the
other's voice is heard in the background.
